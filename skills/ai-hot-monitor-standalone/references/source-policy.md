# Source Policy

- Search international sources with both Chinese and English queries when useful.
- Prefer primary sources and dated reporting.
- Bing RSS is personal non-commercial aggregation only; do not redistribute.
- DuckDuckGo `/html` is disallowed; do not request it.
- Bilibili/X endpoints may require cookies or keys; report failures instead of bypassing access controls.
- Single source failure must not invalidate other results.
- Keep original URL, publisher, timestamp, region, and language.
