# Verification Rubric

- credible: official source or at least two independent trusted publishers support the same fact.
- suspicious: lookalike domain, unsourced screenshot, copied release, unsupported benchmark, or conflicting sources.
- uncertain: plausible but insufficient evidence.
- unverified: no AI or evidence available.
- Multiple aggregators repeating one article are not independent evidence.
- Authenticity, relevance, and urgency are independent dimensions.
